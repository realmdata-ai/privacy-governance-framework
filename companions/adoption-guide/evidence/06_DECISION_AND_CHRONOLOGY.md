## Conditions and authority

The March decision records the per-run epsilon condition of at most 2.0, disclosure of the model-inversion limitation and reassessment before the next calibration. The current snapshot also applies the source's cumulative ledger checks and adopted lifetime and annual caps. This reconstruction does not assert that later versions of the internal cap policy were already in force in March.

The observation boundary and contribution bound must remain valid; parameters and checkpoints stay controlled. Expiry does not erase accounting already incurred. Every additional run needs its own preflight against the current ledger and applicable conditions.

The Processing Owner is the business decider under the applicable delegation. Under the current Medium rule, the CPO concurs and the DPO advises independently. High cases go to the authorised higher-level decider and a separately reasoned G-36 evaluation. These bank-specific titles and arrangements are not universal requirements for adopters.

A register entry, seal, DPO signature or elapsed time cannot authorise something outside a decision's scope. A new purpose, source, recipient or output requires checking current gates and authority before use.
