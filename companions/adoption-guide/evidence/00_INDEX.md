## Traceability rules

A claim points to a record and its source; a gate points to the claims it needs; a decision points to gate conclusions and conditions. A monitoring event points back to the affected claim/condition. A new source dataset, output, recipient, purpose, model or observation channel requires checking that the old record still applies. A hash establishes identity/integrity of a record, not truth or lawful basis. A reader must not promote a teaching record into operational evidence without the underlying work.
