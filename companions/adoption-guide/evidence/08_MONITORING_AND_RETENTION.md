# ADP-08 — Monitoring, change and retention

This teaching plan follows the source's conditions. It does not impose a new programme on PGF.

| What is checked | Role in this case | Trigger and action |
|---|---|---|
| Change of source, feature, purpose, recipient or output | Processing Owner with Legal and Privacy Engineers | Reopen the passport, G-L, triage and relevant assessments before changed processing |
| New generator run or calibration | Processing Owner and Privacy Engineers | Check decision expiry, fresh required evidence, the per-run condition and the lifetime and annual ledger before starting |
| Observable diagnostics, previews or checkpoints | Privacy Engineers | Confirm the observation boundary and account for required expenditure, including failed runs |
| New attack, control failure or changed auxiliary-data assumptions | Privacy Engineering and Processing Owner; DPO advises | Restrict affected use, reassess the scenario or module, log remediation and perform a scoped retest |
| Delta warning or budget failure | Programme owner and business decider under the adopted escalation policy | Report the warning; a budget failure prevents the run and cannot be overridden by discretionary risk acceptance |
| Acceptance expiry | Processing Owner and Privacy Operations | Reassess before continued reliance; make a new decision under current authority |
| Source-extract retention | Data owner, IT and Privacy Engineering | Source example: 30 days after validation; execute deletion, subject to applicable holds and duties |
| Generator parameters | Privacy Engineering | Source example: retain until the synthetic dataset is retired; keep access controlled |
| Synthetic dataset | Data owner and IT | Source example: model version's operational life plus 2 years; assess contextual erasure and interim restriction where required |
| Personal information in an evidence pack | Privacy Operations, Legal and IT | Minimise and restrict it; apply scoped retention and deletion; sealing does not prevent lawful purge |

Monthly disposal reconciliation does not extend a retention period or a right's deadline. Erasure requests are assessed against the current artefact and applicable grounds. Excluding someone from a future run is not sufficient where current data must be erased. No retention period in this teaching case is a universal legal requirement.

The source does not supply a separate executed deletion log for this reconstruction. A real evidence bundle must add the actual validation date, computed due dates and disposal or restriction evidence; this companion does not invent completed disposal.

Issue closure needs evidence of action and appropriate review, not merely a changed status. Monitoring and assurance records distinguish what was measured, what is assumed and what remains untested. The DPO monitors independently; business decisions stay with the authorised decider.
