The source records its reason for not seeking data-subject views on this particular training-data DPIA: no direct decision about individuals is made at this stage; views were sought on the production-scoring design separately. An adopter must assess and document its own consultation choices. This reason and the fictional legal conclusions are not universally reusable.

If a legal prerequisite is confirmed missing, a prohibition applies or required prior consultation has not been completed, scoring cannot cure it: stop or redesign the affected processing. A disputed interpretation is documented with its reasons, assumptions, alternatives, Legal/DPO advice and review date; it is not automatically a confirmed G-L failure. The designated Legal function records whether the proposed design has a supportable basis. Unknown source or recipient facts remain visible and must not be replaced by an invented favourable conclusion.
