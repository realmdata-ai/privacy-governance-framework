# Adoption checklist

Preview 0.2.0-preview.1 · 29 October 2026 · Apply to actual facts and applicable law; this is not a certification checklist.

The adoption lead records an owner, evidence reference, status and next action against each row. Use **done / open / not applicable with reasons**. An unchecked or unknown legal requirement is not satisfied by choosing a low internal risk score. Phases organise adoption work; duties that already apply continue to apply.

| Check | Evidence to inspect | Exit criterion or action |
|---|---|---|
| Scope and purpose are bounded | Activity description, proposed change, people and output list | Everyone can state what is and is not authorised |
| Applicable regimes and duties are mapped | Jurisdiction and role map; PGF 07 adaptation | Unknown applicability has an owner and appropriate restrictions |
| Controller/processor roles and recipients are identified | Contracts, flows, access locations and recipient register | Each role and cross-border flow is assessed on the facts |
| A Processing Owner is designated | Mandate and decision delegation; PGF 02/05/25 | One accountable business role and a known escalation route |
| Programme coordination is resourced | Existing roles or proportionate staffing plan | Responsibilities are performed without assuming a new CPO/committee is compulsory |
| DPO applicability, independence and resources are assessed | Appointment/conflict assessment where required | Statutory monitoring and advice are independent of business decisions |
| Notices, rights, incidents and withdrawal work | Actual routes, records, logs and staff instructions | Statutory actions can be taken now, within their applicable clocks |
| Classification and retention are operational | Data inventory, schedule, deletion/restriction evidence | Technical cycles do not extend legal or adopted limits |
| Data protection by design precedes launch | Design review and defaults | New processing has appropriate controls before use |
| Legal basis and purpose compatibility are documented | G-L and associated analyses | No unresolved legal failure is treated as acceptable risk |
| DPIA necessity is recorded | Facts, statutory/list criteria and triage | Required DPIA happens before the relevant processing |
| Transfers follow their applicable route | Valid mechanism/coverage check and appropriate assessment | No geography-only or synthetic-data exemption is assumed |
| Project passport and tests match real observations | Privacy unit, adversary, auxiliary data, outputs, thresholds and test scope | Required evidence is defined before results are used |
| Missing evidence is classified | Weighted-input coverage, diagnostic limitations, claim evidence and release conditions | No tier without valid selected weighted inputs; no missing test recorded as PASS; diagnostic gaps do not automatically override the tier |
| Risk calculation is traceable | Raw inputs, intermediate results, matrix and scenario decisions | Ordinal scores are not called calibrated probabilities |
| Utility and organisational consequences stay distinct | Separate reports/track (b) | They cannot offset a failed gate or rights-and-freedoms harm |
| G-36 is separately reasoned for each DPIA | Legal conclusion with DPO advice and reasons | Required consultation completed before start/resumption; elapsed time is not approval |
| The decider has current authority | Delegation, version/date and one decision record | Record benefits, alternatives, organisational consequences, DPO/Legal advice and the response; historic titles are not applied before they existed |
| Observations remain separate from the tier | Individual comparisons, uncertainty, calculation and decision | A diagnostic FAIL does not automatically raise the tier; adopted legal, budget and release constraints are checked separately |
| Legal uncertainty is reasoned | Position, assumptions, alternatives, advice and review date | Distinguish uncertainty from an established prohibition or missing legal prerequisite |
| Conditions, expiry and scope are explicit | Decision and risk-register link | Reassessment precedes changed or out-of-scope use |
| DP accounting is complete where relied on | Population/unit, accountant, overlaps, observers and lifetime ledger | Observable failed runs are included; no reset by year/name/version |
| Evidence is controlled and minimised | Index, integrity/access records and retention | Sealing does not block lawful deletion of personal components |
| Monitoring detects material change | Owner/trigger/action table and issues log | New outputs/attacks/recipients/expiry reopen the right assessment |
| Reporting is honest | Numerators, denominators, cut-offs, zero-case and case-level statutory events | Green aggregates do not conceal individual statutory failures |
| Independent review is proportionate | DPO monitoring and suitable independent assurance arrangements | Assurance scope and limits are stated without transferring controller accountability |

## Sign-off record for an adoption increment

Record the increment's scope, completed records, open restrictions, effective decisions, roles, next review and supporting evidence. Sign-off means only that the stated increment meets its exit criteria. It is not blanket permission for every activity in the organisation or for an unassessed future change.
