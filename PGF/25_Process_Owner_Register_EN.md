# PGF-25 — Process Owner Register

*Privacy Governance Framework (PGF) · Level 3 — Process Model · Release 1.0.0-rc.2 · Regulatory reference date: 26 October 2026*

> **Status and use.** Part A is a reusable template. Part B is a worked example for a fictional organisation (PanEuropean Bank AG); the named implementation profile identifies a real product; the Bank, deployment, figures, dates and records in Part B are illustrative. Nothing in this document is legal advice or a statement about any real organisation, product or provider.

# **About This Document**

**Purpose.** Document 25 gives a compact view of who is accountable for each process and sub-process of the Privacy Governance Process Map (Document 24), for escalation, reporting and audit, and keeps the register of Processing Owner mandate holders (Section 10).

**Scope.** Every process and sub-process of Document 24 — the governing (G1–G9), core (C1–C5) and supporting (S1–S9) process groups, the sub-rows of the RACI Matrix (Document 5) and the sub-processes added by the process map (G4.1, G4.2, G5.1, C2.1, C2.2, C2.3, S6.3) — and the statutory activities of Document 5.

**Relationships.** The register is a projection of the RACI Matrix (Document 5); it creates no allocation of responsibility of its own. "Process owner" means the role that is Accountable ("A") in Document 5 (Document 24, Glossary); there is no separate "owner badge". Specific post holders are recorded in the Operational Role and Staffing Model (Document 26); Processing Owner mandate holders are recorded here, in Section 10, as the output of process G9.

# **How to Use This Document**

Part A is a template. Text in ***[square brackets, bold italic]*** is a fillable or illustrative placeholder. Blocks marked **Guidance** are removed from the adopted version. Part B shows the register generated for the fictional PanEuropean Bank AG from its RACI Matrix (version 4.0) and process map (version 2.0).

> **Guidance.** Before generating the register, confirm that the RACI Matrix (Document 5) has been adopted, at least in draft, for every process and sub-process of the map (Document 24). The register cannot be generated without it. If the register and the RACI Matrix differ, the RACI Matrix prevails and the register is regenerated.

## **1. Purpose of This Document**

This document gives a compact view of accountability for each process and sub-process of the process map (Document 24) at ***[organisation name]***, for escalation, reporting to Executive Management and the Governing Body, and audit. It does not redefine the allocation of responsibility, which is fixed by the RACI Matrix (Document 5); it is a projection of that matrix.

> **Guidance.** A register maintained independently of the RACI Matrix creates two sources for one fact. The register is updated only by regenerating it from the current version of Document 5, never by editing entries.

## **2. Scope**

* The register covers every process and sub-process of the process map (Document 24, Sections 2.1 and 2.3), including the sub-rows of Document 5 and the statutory activities (Document 24, Section 2.4).
* Where Document 5 assigns "A" activity by activity (C2, C4), the register lists the decisive activities and their single "A"; for the full allocation, consult Document 5.
* The register names roles, not people. People holding positions are recorded in the Operational Role and Staffing Model (Document 26); Processing Owner mandate holders are recorded in Section 10.

## **3. Register Generation Principle**

* Each entry projects a row of the RACI Matrix: the "R" column shows the Responsible role(s); the "A" column the single Accountable role — the process owner; the "D" column the body that formally adopts or decides where different from "A"; the "C\* / key C" column the mandatory consultations and the main consulted roles; the "Escalation" column where an unresolved issue goes.
* The source versions of Document 5 and Document 24 are recorded in the header. A discrepancy between the register and the RACI Matrix means the register has not been regenerated; it is never an alternative allocation.
* There is no separate "owner badge". Where Document 5 splits a process into sub-rows with different Accountable roles (for example S7.1–S7.5), each sub-row has its own owner and there is no owner of the process as a whole.
* Update triggers: a change to the processes or sub-processes of Document 24; a change to any R, A or D in Document 5; a change in the organisational structure of the privacy function (Document 4); a designation under G9 (Section 10).

## **7. Use of the Register**

* **Escalation.** The register is the first checkpoint for "to whom does an unresolved issue on process X go", before the full RACI Matrix is consulted. The escalation contacts include the DPO's published contact details (Art. 37(7)) for matters within the DPO's tasks.
* **Reporting.** The Governance Reporting Template (Document 31) uses the register to show who is accountable for following up decisions of Executive Management and the Governing Body.
* **Audit.** The Privacy Audit and Monitoring Programme (Document 27) uses the register to identify the addressee of an audit request for each process; Internal Audit's own accountability (G6.2a, G6.2b) is shown here but its independence is not affected.

## **9. Relationship to Other Framework Documents**

* Is a projection of the RACI Matrix (Document 5), the single source of truth for the allocation of responsibility.
* Covers the processes and sub-processes of the Privacy Governance Process Map (Document 24, Sections 2.1, 2.3 and 2.4); "process owner" has the meaning of the Document 24 glossary.
* Uses the residual-risk tiers and acceptance authority of the Privacy Risk Appetite Statement (Document 8) without redefining them.
* Is used by the Governance Reporting Template (Document 31) and the Privacy Audit and Monitoring Programme (Document 27).
* Does not replace the Operational Role and Staffing Model (Document 26), which maps roles to filled positions.
* Is distinct from the Processing Owner mandate (Charter, Section 8; role profile — Document 6, Section 4.6). "Process owner" in Sections 4–6 is the Accountable role for a process; a Processing Owner is the business owner of a processing activity, designated under G9 and recorded in Section 10. The mandate creates no staff position and is therefore not recorded in Document 26.
* Section 10 is the output of process G9 (Document 24, Section 4.10).

## **3. Register Generation Principle**

The Privacy Operations Lead regenerates the register on the GRC system from the RACI Matrix and the process map, records their versions in the header and publishes it after the CPO's confirmation. Update triggers: a change to the map, to the RACI allocation (for example on entry into a new country) or to the structure of the privacy function, and every G9 designation.

# **International Adaptation**

> **Guidance.** The register structure is jurisdiction-neutral. The applicability map for Canada is in Document 7, Section 3.1.

* **United Kingdom and Switzerland (headline).** The same projection applies; for Switzerland, where a data protection advisor is appointed (revFADP Art. 10), show the advisor's rows in place of the DPO's.
* **Canada — PIPEDA.** The individual accountable for compliance under Principle 4.1 (the "Privacy Officer") is typically the EPPO and may be "A" for programme rows; that person is not an EU DPO, and the DPO-only rows (G4.1(b), G6.1, G8.2, S8.1) are re-assigned or removed according to the organisation's design.
* **Canada — Québec (P-39.1).** The person exercising the highest authority is the person in charge of the protection of personal information by default and may delegate the function in writing; record the delegation as a G9-style entry in Section 10.
