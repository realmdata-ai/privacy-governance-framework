# PGF-30 — Privacy Awareness and Training Programme

*Privacy Governance Framework (PGF) · Level 4 — Control, Reporting and Improvement · Release 1.0.0-rc.1 · Regulatory reference date: 26 October 2026*

> **Status and use.** Part A is a reusable template. Part B is a worked example for a fictional organisation (PanEuropean Bank AG); all names, figures, dates and records in Part B are illustrative. Nothing in this document is legal advice or a statement about any real organisation, product or provider.

# **About This Document**

**Purpose.** Document 30 sets out the Privacy Awareness and Training Programme: baseline training for everyone who handles personal data on the organisation's behalf, including contractors; training of the management body and of Processing Owners; role-specific programmes for privacy roles; AI literacy; awareness-raising between training cycles; and how completion and effectiveness are tracked.

**Scope.** Employees, members of the management body, Processing Owners, contractors and temporary staff with access to personal data, and the holders of privacy roles. The DPO's own awareness-raising (GDPR Art. 39(1)(b)) and the maintenance of the DPO's expertise (Art. 38(2)) are covered at their interfaces.

**Relationships.** The Privacy Role Competency Framework (Document 6, Section 6) sets what each role must know and how often it is reconfirmed; this document delivers it. The "Staff Training and Awareness" process (Document 24, Section 6.10; Document 5, rows S8 and S8.1) runs it. Metrics are defined in Document 28 (Section 6.4); escalation follows Document 31, Section 6.4.

## **1. Purpose of the Document**

This document is Document 30 of the Privacy Governance Framework (PGF), the fourth document of Level 4 "Control, Reporting and Improvement". It sets the baseline training for all persons acting under the organisation's authority who handle personal data (Section 5), awareness-raising activities between training cycles (Section 6), programmes for the management body, Processing Owners and privacy roles (Section 7), frequencies (Section 8), and the tracking of completion and effectiveness (Section 9).

It supports several legal and supervisory expectations: persons acting under the authority of the controller process personal data only on its instructions (GDPR Arts. 29, 32(4)); the DPO monitors awareness-raising and training of staff (Art. 39(1)(b)); for financial entities, ICT security awareness and digital operational resilience training are compulsory for all employees and senior management (DORA Art. 13(6)) and members of the management body keep their knowledge up to date, including through specific training (DORA Art. 5(4)); for entities in scope of NIS2, members of management bodies follow training (NIS2 Art. 20(2)); and providers and deployers of AI systems take measures to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy of the persons dealing with those systems on their behalf (AI Act Art. 4, in the version in force — Document 7 records its current wording).

The Privacy Role Competency Framework (Document 6) sets what is required of each role; this document sets how it is delivered. It does not redefine those requirements or create roles. Awareness activities (Section 6) are not a means of confirming qualification and are not tracked per person.

## **1. Purpose of the Document**

This document applies the Privacy Awareness and Training Programme to PanEuropean Bank AG as at 23 October 2026. It sets the baseline programme for all staff and in-scope contractors, the Management Board and Processing Owner programmes, the role-specific modules, AI literacy and the awareness loop.

For PanEuropean, as a financial entity, the privacy content is coordinated with the compulsory ICT security awareness and digital operational resilience training run by the CISO and ICT Risk Control (DORA Art. 13(6)), and with the Management Board's ICT-risk training (DORA Art. 5(4)). ## **2. Scope**

## **3. Principles**

* Needs-based: the 2027 needs analysis (planned, December 2026) draws on breach root causes (for example, BR-2026-0117, a statement letter sent to the wrong recipient), the Group DPO's monitoring findings, complaints and the AI Act and DORA duties
* Awareness supplements training: reading the monthly "Privacy Pulse" does not count as completion of the baseline programme
* Effectiveness: knowledge-check results and human-factor incidents before and after training are analysed annually (Section 9)
* Proportionate processing of staff data: completion is recorded per person only for mandatory training; newsletter reach is measured in aggregate; in Germany, the processing of training records is governed by a works agreement with the works council (illustrative), in line with the co-determination rules for technical monitoring of employees
* Vendor-neutral qualifications: "a recognised privacy certification or equivalent demonstrable expertise"; IAPP credentials are examples only
* No sanction on the Group DPO: a lapse triggers a funded catch-up plan, never suspension
* Content accuracy: General Counsel reviews the legal content of all modules every September and on regulatory change

## **9. Delivery, Completion Tracking and Effectiveness**

* Completion is recorded in the PanEuropean Learning Hub with date and check result; a missed due date is non-completion, not an extension
* Delivery follows process S8: content by the Privacy Operations Lead, approval by the Chief Privacy Officer, delivery and tracking by HR PanEuropean, evaluation by the Privacy Operations Lead
* Effectiveness is evaluated each November from knowledge-check results (M-S8-04), human-factor breaches before and after training (M-S8-03), aggregate phishing-simulation results and the Group DPO's monitoring findings
* Metrics reported monthly to the dashboard: M-S8-01, M-S8-05 to M-S8-09 and M-S8.1-01 (Document 28)
* Learning Hub records are retained under the bank's HR and supervisory record-keeping rules for evidence of training

## **10. Escalation for Non-Completion of Training**

Addressees and time limits follow Document 31, Part B, Section 6.4.

| Level | Description |
| :---- | :---- |
| Low | Completion due within 30 days; no delay |
| Medium | Due date missed by up to 30 days — reminder through the Learning Hub, module reassigned |
| High | Delay of more than 30 days for a holder of a privacy role, a Processing Owner or a Management Board member; or a repeated delay by another person |
| Critical | More than 20% of a business unit overdue (for example, Digital Banking); or the Management Board briefing not held within 12 months |

* An individual delay is never Critical
* For roles other than the Group DPO and the Deputy Group DPO, a delegated authorisation that depends on the lapsed qualification may be paused until the gap is closed — for example, a Privacy Engineer's independent configuration of PET techniques, or a Processing Owner's authority to accept at Medium; the decision then passes to the next level of the delegation (Document 8, Section 5)
* The Group DPO and the Deputy Group DPO are never suspended for a training or certification lapse; a funded catch-up plan applies
* Disciplinary matters follow the employment law of the country concerned, the bank's HR policies and applicable co-determination rights
