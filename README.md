# Privacy Governance Framework (PGF)

**Version 1.0.0 - first publication package, 28 October 2026**

Authors: **Alexey Neyman; Val Khvatov**. Copyright (c) 2026 **REALM INVEO**. Documentation licence: [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). Regulatory reference date: 26 October 2026.

PGF consists of an Overview and 31 components in four levels, each with reusable templates and worked examples for a fictional bank. It helps organisations connect privacy requirements, business objectives, risk calculations, evidence and accountable decisions.

- [Overview](PGF/00_Framework_Overview_EN.md)
- [Risk methodology](PGF/18_Privacy_Risk_Assessment_Methodology_EN.md) and [risk appetite](PGF/08_Privacy_Risk_Appetite_Statement_EN.md)
- [PAMOLA implementation profile](IMPLEMENTATION_PROFILE.md)
- [Glossary](PGF/GLOSSARY.md), [standards crosswalk](PGF/STANDARDS_CROSSWALK.md), [official references](references/README.md)
- [Approval and rights](AUTHOR_APPROVAL.md), [release status](PGF/RELEASE_READINESS.md)

The method is unchanged from the [rc.2 content freeze](https://github.com/realmdata-ai/privacy-governance-framework/tree/freeze-pgf-1.0.0-rc.2-2026-10-28). The integrated score maps directly to a band and matrix result; individual observations/DPO dissent accompany the business decision without automatic tier overrides. Legal prerequisites, valid inputs and claims, and adopted release conditions remain separate.
